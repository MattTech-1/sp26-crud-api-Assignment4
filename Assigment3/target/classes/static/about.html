About Page

🎮 Welcome to the Nintendo Character Gallery!

This website celebrates some of Nintendo's most iconic characters, from Mario to Link. These characters have shaped gaming history and continue to inspire fans of all ages.

💡 Purpose

The Nintendo Character Gallery was created to highlight and share the history of Nintendo's most well-known characters. Inspired by growing up with these games, this project combines nostalgia with useful information for both longtime fans and newcomers.

🎨 Design Approach

  • Colors: Red, white, and gray inspired by the Nintendo brand
  • Layout: Card-based design for simple and easy browsing
  • Features: Search and hover effects for better interaction
  • Typography:
    • Clean, readable, and slightly playful fonts

⭐ Featured Characters
